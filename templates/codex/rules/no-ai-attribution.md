# No AI attribution

Never add `Co-authored-by:`, `Generated-by:`, `Assisted-by:`, `Signed-off-by: <AI>`,
"Generated with …", "Made with Cursor/Codex/Claude", or similar AI attribution to
commit messages, `git commit --trailer`, `-m` bodies, PR/MR titles or descriptions,
squash messages, tags, changelogs, or other generated text. Do not pass such
trailers to `gh`, `glab`, or other tools. Keep user-authored trailers as written.
